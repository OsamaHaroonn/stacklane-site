Where the numbers come from

No magic — just arithmetic you can check

admin cost = hours/week × weeks × hourly value lost sales = ignored enquiries × weeks × conversion × job value monthly leak = admin cost + lost sales

The conversion default (30%) is a deliberately cautious share of people who contact you and never hear back. Change it — you know your own follow-up better than any default.

Common questions

Before you ask

How is the monthly leak calculated?

Admin cost + lost sales, using the formula printed above. Nothing is hidden and nothing is estimated on our side — every input is yours.

Is the 30% conversion guess too high?

Possibly — that is why the page also shows the result at 10%. If the number still matters at 10%, the problem is real whatever the exact percentage is.

Do you store my numbers?

No. Everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and the page sets no cookies. The only thing that leaves your device is a link you choose to copy.

My leak is small — should I buy anything to fix it?

No. Under about $500 a month the page says so plainly and tells you to fix your follow-up habit first, which is free. The paid kit only earns its money when the leak is far larger than its price.
