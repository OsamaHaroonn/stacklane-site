Refund policy

One rule, applied the same way every time: if it isn't useful, you get your money back.

The Kit — 14 days, no questions beyond one

Email within 14 days of purchase and we refund the full amount. We ask what went wrong only because it makes the next version better — you never have to explain, produce a reason, or fill in a form. Refunds are returned to the original payment method, normally within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Services — you see it before you pay

Every build starts with a working preview you approve before payment, so most problems are caught before money changes hands.

  • Before the build starts: cancel any time, no charge.
  • After a deposit, before handover: if we miss an agreed milestone through our fault, you choose a fix, a reduced price, or a refund of the unearned balance.
  • After handover: work already delivered isn't refundable, but the 14-day window still covers defects in what we shipped — we fix them.
