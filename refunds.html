Refund policy

One rule, applied the same way every time: if it isn't useful, you get your money back within 14 days.

How you actually get the money back

Refunds run through the platform you bought from — it is the one that holds your payment. Open the receipt email from Lemon Squeezy, Paddle or Gumroad (search your inbox or spam for that platform's name) and use the refund link inside it, or the "request a refund" action in that platform's customer portal. One click, no form on this site, no interrogation.

  • Window: 14 days from purchase.
  • Reason: you don't have to give one. If you do, it is used to improve the product, nothing else.
  • Speed: approved refunds are issued by the platform immediately and normally appear in your account within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
  • Currency: returned in the currency you paid.

If you can't find the receipt

Use the support form on the home page and include the email address you paid with and, if you have it, the order reference. When a support form is not shown, the platform's own customer portal — "already a customer" on the checkout platform's site — recovers any purchase by email address. We never ask for card numbers, ID documents or bank details, and neither should anyone claiming to act for this site.

Free tools

Free, with nothing to refund. Use them as much as you like. If one behaves wrongly, the support form on the home page is the place to say so — they run entirely in your browser, so a reload usually fixes the rest.

What is not refundable

Nothing else is sold here. There is no subscription, no service engagement and no recurring charge to cancel — the only thing you can buy is a one-time download.
