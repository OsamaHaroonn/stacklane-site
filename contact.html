Reporting a problem

There is no contact form on this page, and that is deliberate. A form needs a server to receive it, and this site has none — it is a folder of files.

Why there is no form

Every tool here runs in your browser. Adding a form would mean adding the one thing the rest of the site avoids: a place where your details leave your device and arrive somewhere else. The pages also carry a Content Security Policy that refuses form submissions, so a form on this page would not be able to send anything even if it were here.

If a tool gives a result you think is wrong

Nothing is logged and nothing is reported back automatically, so a problem can only be fixed if it can be reproduced. Worth writing down:

  • The page: its name and the address in your browser.
  • The exact input: the numbers, text or file you used, if you can share it.
  • What the tool showed, and what you expected instead.
  • Your browser and whether you were on a phone or a desktop.

Each tool's own page also lists its assumptions and limits, which is often enough to explain a difference: for example a calculator that ignores fees, or a word counter that treats a hyphenated word as one word.
