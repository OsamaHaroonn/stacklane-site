If the file hasn't arrived

Three things to try, in order

01

Look for the platform's email

Search your inbox for Lemon Squeezy, Paddle or Gumroad — whichever you checked out through. Check spam and promotions, and search for the address you paid with.

02

Open the customer portal

Every platform has a receipt page with your files and your order history. Use the link inside that email, or the "already a customer" option on that platform's site.

03

Refund or something broken

Refunds are one click inside the same receipt, with the same 14-day window as the refund policy. Anything wrong with a free tool on this site runs in your browser — reloading fixes most of it.
