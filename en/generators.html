    Generators

    Create QR codes and strong passwords in your browser.

    What these tools do

    Create QR codes, strong passwords, UUIDs, random numbers and favicons. Generation happens in the browser, so a code or password made here is never transmitted or stored.

    6 tools in this category. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you type or open is uploaded.

    QR code generator

    Create a QR code for a link, text or any message. Generated in your browser, downloadable as a PNG.

    →

    Barcode generator

    Encode text or numbers as a Code 128 barcode drawn as SVG, with the checksum calculated in your browser. Download or copy it.

    →

    Password generator

    Generate a strong random password in your browser, with length and character controls and a live strength estimate.

    →

    Random number generator

    Generate one or more random whole numbers in a range you choose, using the browser's cryptographic random source.

    →

    UUID generator

    Generate version 4 UUIDs in your browser, one at a time or in a batch, with an optional uppercase form.

    →

    Favicon generator

    Turn one image into favicon PNGs at 16, 32, 48, 180, 192 and 512 pixels, drawn on a canvas in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
