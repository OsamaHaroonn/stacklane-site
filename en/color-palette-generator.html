Color palette generator

Type a hex colour and the tool mixes it with white and black to give five swatches with both hex and rgb values, ready to copy.

Runs locally in your browser

How it works

Every swatch is a straight mix of the base colour with either white or black, done per channel with the same formula: new channel equals the base channel multiplied by one minus the mix amount, plus the target channel multiplied by the mix amount, rounded to a whole number. Two tints use white at 50% and 25%, the third swatch is the base untouched, and two shades use black at 25% and 50%. Because only the distance to white or black changes, the hue stays in the same family and the swatches step evenly in lightness, which is what a palette needs. Three-digit hex is expanded first, and values outside 0 to 255 cannot occur because the mix is clamped.

Worked example

For #336699 the channels are 51, 102 and 153. The 50% tint gives 51 × 0.5 + 255 × 0.5 = 153, 178.5 and 204, which rounds to #99B3CC. The 25% tint gives #668CB3, the base is #336699, the 25% shade gives #264D73 and the 50% shade gives #1A334D. Each swatch also reports its rgb values, for example rgb(153, 179, 204) for the first tint.
