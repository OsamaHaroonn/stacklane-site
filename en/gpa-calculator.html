GPA calculator

Enter each course with its grade points and its units, and the tool returns the weighted average, the total units and the quality points behind it.

Runs locally in your browser

How it works

GPA = total quality points ÷ total units. Quality points for one course are its grade points multiplied by its units, so a course worth four units counts twice as much as one worth two — this is why a high mark in a large course moves the average more than a high mark in a small one. The tool adds up the quality points and the units across every row you fill in and divides one by the other. The grade scale is entirely yours: there is no built-in scale, because scales differ between countries, institutions and departments, and claiming one standard would be wrong. Rows left blank are ignored, so you can keep a spare row ready.

Worked example

Three courses: A with 4.0 points for 3 units, B with 3.0 points for 4 units, and A with 4.0 points for 2 units. The quality points are 4.0 × 3 = 12, 3.0 × 4 = 12 and 4.0 × 2 = 8, which add up to 32, and the units are 3 + 4 + 2 = 9. The GPA is 32 ÷ 9 = 3.56.
