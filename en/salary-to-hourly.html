Salary to hourly calculator

Enter a yearly salary plus the hours and weeks you work, and the tool divides it down to an hourly rate with the daily, weekly and monthly equivalents.

Runs locally in your browser

How it works

The hourly rate is the yearly salary divided by the paid hours in a year, and those hours are your hours per week multiplied by your weeks per year. Forty hours over fifty-two weeks is 2,080 hours, so a 52,000 salary gives 25.00 an hour. The weekly figure divides the salary by the number of weeks, the monthly figure divides it by twelve, and the daily figure takes the hourly rate and multiplies it by the weekly hours divided by five. Every result is printed with the arithmetic that produced it, so you can check each line yourself. No tax, pension or holiday rules are applied: the tool only divides the number you type.

Worked example

For a 52,000 salary at 40 hours a week over 52 weeks, the paid hours are 40 × 52 = 2,080. The hourly rate is 52,000 ÷ 2,080 = 25.00. The daily figure is 25.00 × (40 ÷ 5) = 200.00, the weekly figure is 52,000 ÷ 52 = 1,000.00, and the monthly figure is 52,000 ÷ 12 = 4,333.33.
