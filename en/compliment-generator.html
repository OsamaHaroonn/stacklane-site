Compliment generator

Ask for a compliment and the tool puts one together from three small lists of kind, specific phrases, with another available on every press.

Runs locally in your browser

How it works

Each compliment is assembled from three parts: an opening about you, a verb phrase describing something you do, and a closing that says why it matters. One phrase is drawn from each list with the browser's random source, so the combination is new each time even though every part is a fixed, written phrase. The lists hold seven openings, ten verb phrases and six closings, which gives 7 × 10 × 6 = 420 possible lines. Nothing is inferred about the reader and nothing is personalised: the tool does not ask for a name or read anything from the page. The copy button copies the line currently shown, and a new line replaces it rather than piling up a history.

Worked example

With seven openings, ten verb phrases and six closings, the tool can produce 7 × 10 × 6 = 420 different compliments. One draw might give "What stands out is how you" plus "listen properly" plus "and that is rarer than people think.", which reads as one sentence: What stands out is how you listen properly, and that is rarer than people think.
