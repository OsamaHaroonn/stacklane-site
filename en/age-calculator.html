    Age calculator

    Enter a date of birth and see the exact age in years, months and days, plus the total days between that date and today. Everything is calculated in your browser.

    Runs locally in your browser

    How it works

    The calculator subtracts the calendar dates rather than the raw timestamps, so a birthday at 11pm does not lose a day. It walks year by year, then month by month, then counts the remaining days — the same method that handles February in a leap year correctly. The total-days figure counts whole days between the two dates, ignoring the time of day.

    Worked example

    Born 5 March 2000, checked on 28 October 2026: 26 years, 7 months and 23 days — about 9,733 days in total. The year count only increases once the anniversary has passed, so it stays 26 for the whole month of March until the 5th.

    Questions and answers

    Does it handle leap years?

    Yes. Because it works on calendar dates rather than a fixed number of days per year, 29 February birthdays are counted correctly, including people born on a day that does not exist in common years.

    What if the date is in the future?

    The tool refuses it and asks for a date on or before today, instead of showing a negative age.

    Why does the age differ from my birthday on Facebook?

    Some services count a full year only after the whole birth month has passed, or use the time zone the account was created in. This tool counts exact calendar years, months and days.

    Is the total number of days exact?

    It counts whole days between the two dates, so it is exact for dates but does not include the hour of birth. Treat it as a day-level figure.

    Can I use this officially?

    For passports, legal documents or benefits, use the date printed on your official documents. This tool is a quick check, not a legal record.
