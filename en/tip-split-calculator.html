    Tip and split calculator

    Type the bill, the tip percentage and how many people are splitting it to see the tip, the total and each person's share.

    Runs locally in your browser

    How it works

    The tip is the bill multiplied by the tip percentage divided by 100. The total is the bill plus the tip, and each person's share is the total divided by the number of people. Anyone splitting has to pay a whole amount of money, so a fractional head count is rounded up to the next person: splitting between 2.5 people would otherwise show a figure nobody can hand over. The figures are plain numbers with no currency symbol, because the tool cannot know which currency you are paying in.

    Worked example

    A bill of 120 with a 15% tip split between 4 people: the tip is 120 × 15 ÷ 100 = 18, so the total is 120 + 18 = 138. Divided by 4 people each pays 138 ÷ 4 = 34.50.

    Questions and answers

    Why is there no currency symbol?

    Because the tool does not know which currency you are using. The result is in whatever unit you typed, which makes it correct for any currency.

    Why can the tip not go above 100%?

    A tip larger than the bill itself is almost always a typing slip — 1500 entered instead of 15, say. Capping it at 100% turns that mistake into a clear message instead of a nonsense total.

    What happens with a fractional number of people?

    It is rounded up to the next whole person. Splitting 138 between 2.5 people would give 55.20 each, which nobody can pay, so the tool splits between 3 instead.

    Can I calculate a zero tip?

    Yes. Enter 0% and the tip row shows 0, the total equals the bill and the split is the bill divided by the people.

    Does it handle a service charge already added?

    No. Enter the bill with the service charge already included and use the tip percentage only for what you want to add on top, so you do not tip twice.
