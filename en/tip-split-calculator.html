Tip and split calculator

Type the bill, the tip percentage and how many people are splitting it to see the tip, the total and each person's share.

Runs locally in your browser

How it works

The tip is the bill multiplied by the tip percentage divided by 100. The total is the bill plus the tip, and each person's share is the total divided by the number of people. Anyone splitting has to pay a whole amount of money, so a fractional head count is rounded up to the next person: splitting between 2.5 people would otherwise show a figure nobody can hand over. The figures are plain numbers with no currency symbol, because the tool cannot know which currency you are paying in.

Worked example

A bill of 120 with a 15% tip split between 4 people: the tip is 120 × 15 ÷ 100 = 18, so the total is 120 + 18 = 138. Divided by 4 people each pays 138 ÷ 4 = 34.50.
