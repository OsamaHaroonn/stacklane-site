Margin and markup, spelled out

profit = price − cost margin = profit ÷ price (share of the sale that is yours) markup = profit ÷ cost (how much you added on top of cost) price at target margin = cost ÷ (1 − target/100)

A $600 cost sold for $1,500 is 60% margin but 150% markup. Quoting "150% margin" to a client who checks would be embarrassing — this tool tells you which one you're holding.
