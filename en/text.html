    Text

    Count, check and transform text you paste in.

    What these tools do

    Count words and characters, change letter case, remove repeated lines, sort a list, compare two drafts, generate placeholder text or measure typing speed. Paste your text and the result updates as you type.

    10 tools in this category. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you type or open is uploaded.

    Word counter

    Count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in any text, with an estimated reading time. Nothing leaves your browser.

    →

    Case converter

    Change text between upper case, lower case, title case, sentence case, camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case.

    →

    Character counter

    Count characters, characters without spaces, words, lines, sentences, letters, digits and the UTF-8 size of your text.

    →

    Remove duplicate lines

    Delete repeated lines from a list or a block of text, keeping the first occurrence and the original order.

    →

    Typing speed test

    Measure your typing speed in words per minute and your accuracy against a fixed sample, with the formula and a running timer shown.

    →

    Lorem ipsum generator

    Generate placeholder text as paragraphs, sentences or single words from a built-in word list, with a chosen amount and the option to start with Lorem ipsum.

    →

    Text diff checker

    Compare two versions of a text line by line. The checker marks added, removed and unchanged lines, runs entirely in your browser and never uploads a word.

    →

    Slug generator

    Turn any heading into a clean URL slug: lowercased, accents removed, symbols stripped and the separator you choose. Runs in your browser and nothing is saved.

    →

    Sort lines

    Sort lines of text A to Z, Z to A, numerically or by length, with an option to remove empty lines. Everything runs in your browser and input is capped.

    →

    Keyword density analyzer

    Paste text to see how often each word appears, with counts and percentages from a stated formula. Up to 50,000 characters, nothing uploaded.
