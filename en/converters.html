    Convert units, time zones, number bases, Roman numerals, fractions and ratios. Conversions use fixed, published factors, and the page names the factor whenever a result depends on one.

    6 tools in this category. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you type or open is uploaded.

    Unit converter

    Convert length, mass, volume, temperature and speed between metric and imperial units, with the exact factor shown.

    Time zone converter

    Convert a date and time between time zones using your browser's own time-zone data, with the UTC offset and daylight saving shown for each zone.
