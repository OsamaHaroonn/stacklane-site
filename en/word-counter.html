    Text

    Word counter

    Paste or type any text and get an instant count of words, characters, sentences and paragraphs, plus an estimated reading time. The text stays in your browser.

    Runs locally in your browser

    How it works

    The text box is split on whitespace to count words, then scanned for sentence-ending punctuation and blank lines to count sentences and paragraphs. Characters are counted twice: once exactly as typed, and once with spaces removed. Reading time is the word count divided by about 200 words per minute, a rough average for prose — treat it as an estimate, not a measurement.

    Worked example

    The sentence “Send the invoice on Friday.” has 5 words, 33 characters including the space after “the”, 31 without spaces, 1 sentence and 1 paragraph. At 200 words per minute it reads in under a second.

    Questions and answers

    Is my text uploaded anywhere?

    No. Counting happens in your browser with JavaScript. The text box is never sent to a server, and nothing is stored.

    How exactly is a word counted?

    A word is any run of characters separated by whitespace. “well-known” and “don’t” each count as one word; punctuation attached to a word is counted with it.

    Why might the sentence count look wrong?

    Sentences are counted by splitting on . ! ? and similar marks. Abbreviations such as “e.g.” or decimals such as “3.5” can be counted as sentence ends, so treat the figure as an estimate.

    What does reading time assume?

    It divides the word count by 200 words per minute. Technical, dense or second-language reading is slower; skimming is faster. It is a guide, not a guarantee.

    Does it work for Arabic or Chinese?

    For languages written with spaces, yes. For Chinese and Japanese, which do not use spaces, the character count is the more useful figure. The character count is always shown.
