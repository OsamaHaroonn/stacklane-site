Word counter

Paste or type any text and get an instant count of words, characters, sentences and paragraphs, plus an estimated reading time. The text stays in your browser.

Runs locally in your browser

How it works

The text box is split on whitespace to count words, then scanned for sentence-ending punctuation and blank lines to count sentences and paragraphs. Characters are counted twice: once exactly as typed, and once with spaces removed. Reading time is the word count divided by about 200 words per minute, a rough average for prose — treat it as an estimate, not a measurement.

Worked example

The sentence “Send the invoice on Friday.” has 5 words, 33 characters including the space after “the”, 31 without spaces, 1 sentence and 1 paragraph. At 200 words per minute it reads in under a second.
