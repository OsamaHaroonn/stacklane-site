Break-even, spelled out

profit per job = price − variable cost jobs to break even = fixed costs ÷ profit per job revenue at break even = jobs × price

Round up: you can't sell 2.7 jobs, so 3 is the real answer. The calculator rounds for you and shows the raw figure too.
