    Sort lines

    Paste a list and sort it A to Z, Z to A, by number or by line length, with an option to drop empty lines. The result is a text block you can copy.

    Runs locally in your browser

    How it works

    The text is split on line breaks, and one trailing line break counts as an ending rather than a blank line. A to Z compares whole lines character by character, so digits sort before capitals because the code for 1 is lower than the code for A, and Z to A reverses that order. Numeric sorting parses each line as a number and pushes lines that are not numbers to the end in their original order, with a warning that counts them. Sorting by length compares character counts and keeps the original order between equal lengths. The remove-empty option drops blank lines before any sort. Input is capped at 50,000 characters and 5,000 lines.

    Worked example

    The four lines pear, Apple, 10 and banana sort to 10, Apple, banana, pear from A to Z, because digits are coded lower than capitals, and to pear, banana, Apple, 10 from Z to A. By length they become 10, pear, Apple, banana, since 10 has 2 characters, pear has 4, Apple has 5 and banana has 6. A numeric sort of 10, -2 and 3.5 gives -2, 3.5, 10, and any word left in that list is named in a warning instead of being sorted by mistake.

    Questions and answers

    Why do numbers sort before letters?

    A to Z compares character codes, and the digit 1 is coded lower than the capital A, so 10 comes first. Use the numeric order when the lines are numbers.

    What happens to blank lines?

    They keep their place by default. Tick remove empty lines and they are dropped before the sort runs.

    What about non-numeric lines in a numeric sort?

    They are placed at the end in the order you typed them, and a warning tells you how many there were.

    Is there a size limit?

    Yes: 50,000 characters and 5,000 lines. Larger input is refused with a clear message rather than freezing the page.

    Is my list stored?

    No. Sorting happens in the browser, nothing is written to storage and nothing is sent over the network.
