Savings goal calculator

Enter the amount you want, what you already have, an interest rate and the number of years to see how much you still need and the monthly deposit that gets you there.

Runs locally in your browser

How it works

What you already have grows at the monthly rate for the number of months you entered, using FV = C × (1 + i)^N where i is the annual rate divided by 100 and then by 12 and N is the number of months. The gap left is the target minus that figure. The monthly deposit needed to close the gap is PMT = gap × i ÷ ((1 + i)^N − 1), the standard annuity formula for payments made at the end of each month. At a 0% rate it simplifies to the gap divided by the number of months. The assumptions are stated because they matter: one fixed rate for the whole period, interest added every month, no withdrawals, and a deposit at the end of every month without fail. Results are an estimate from those assumptions, not financial advice.

Worked example

A goal of 10,000 with 2,000 already saved, 4% a year over 5 years: i = 0.04 ÷ 12 = 0.003333 and N = 60. The 2,000 grows to 2,000 × 1.003333^60 = 2,441.99, leaving 10,000 − 2,441.99 = 7,558.01 to find. The monthly deposit is 7,558.01 × 0.003333 ÷ (1.003333^60 − 1) = 114.00. With no interest the same goal would need (10,000 − 2,000) ÷ 60 = 133.33 a month.
