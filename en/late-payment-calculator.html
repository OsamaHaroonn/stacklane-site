What is being charged, and on what basis

daily interest = amount × (annual rate ÷ 100) ÷ 365 interest to date = daily interest × days overdue late fee to date = amount × (monthly rate ÷ 100) × (days ÷ 30) total owed = amount + interest + late fee + admin fee

The fee is prorated by days rather than charged in blocks, so a 10-day delay doesn't cost a whole month's fee.
