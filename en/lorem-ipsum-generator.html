    Text

    Lorem ipsum generator

    Choose paragraphs, sentences or words, set how many, and copy the placeholder text. Everything is generated in your browser from a fixed word list.

    Runs locally in your browser

    How it works

    The generator draws words from a built-in list of sixty-three Latin-looking words and assembles them into sentences and paragraphs. Sentence mode returns exactly the number you ask for, paragraph mode groups three to five sentences per paragraph, and word mode returns the count directly. The first word of each sentence is capitalised and the sentence ends with a full stop, so the output reads as prose. With the lorem-ipsum switch on, the text begins with the traditional opening instead of a random word. Counts are capped at 10 paragraphs, 30 sentences and 200 words, which keeps the page responsive. There is no network request and no history: the text exists only in this tab until you copy it.

    Worked example

    Asking for three sentences produces three sentences, so the output contains exactly three full stops and ends with one. Asking for five words produces exactly five words, with no full stop unless the five words happen to form a complete sentence. Asking for two paragraphs produces two blocks separated by a blank line, each with three to five sentences.

    Questions and answers

    Is this real Latin?

    No, it is a scrambled placeholder based on a passage from Cicero. It has no reliable meaning, which is the point: readers notice the layout instead of reading the words.

    Why is the text different each time?

    Words are picked in random order from the built-in list, so two presses give different results. Only the opening phrase is fixed when you turn that switch on.

    Can I choose the amount exactly?

    Yes for sentences and words. Paragraphs vary in length by design, three to five sentences each, so a paragraph count gives you that many blocks rather than an exact sentence total.

    Is the generated text saved?

    No. It is held in the page and copy is the only way out. There is no storage, account or network request.

    Why is there a maximum?

    The caps keep the tab quick and the output readable. Requests above 10 paragraphs, 30 sentences or 200 words are refused with the allowed range in the message.
