How it works

The generator draws words from a built-in list of sixty-three Latin-looking words and assembles them into sentences and paragraphs. Sentence mode returns exactly the number you ask for, paragraph mode groups three to five sentences per paragraph, and word mode returns the count directly. The first word of each sentence is capitalised and the sentence ends with a full stop, so the output reads as prose. With the lorem-ipsum switch on, the text begins with the traditional opening instead of a random word. Counts are capped at 10 paragraphs, 30 sentences and 200 words, which keeps the page responsive. There is no network request and no history: the text exists only in this tab until you copy it.

Worked example

Asking for three sentences produces three sentences, so the output contains exactly three full stops and ends with one. Asking for five words produces exactly five words, with no full stop unless the five words happen to form a complete sentence. Asking for two paragraphs produces two blocks separated by a blank line, each with three to five sentences.
