Privacy policy

Short version: this site is built so that it has almost nothing to collect. The long version is below.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies — not for ads, not for analytics, not for "preferences".
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts.
  • No accounts, no login, no database of visitors.
  • No advertising or data brokers, because there is no data to share.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information, ever.

What stays on your device

The free tools (invoice generator, calculators, audit checklist) save your drafts in your own browser's local storage so a refresh doesn't erase your work. That data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Nothing is uploaded — you can disconnect from the internet and the tools keep working.

The support form

If a support form appears on the home page, it posts to a form service that accepts messages on this site's behalf. That means your message is delivered through that service's infrastructure. You choose what to put in it — nothing on this site fills it in for you. If no form is shown, the site has no contact inbox at all and support runs entirely through your payment receipt.

Payments

The Kit is paid for through a third-party checkout platform. Card numbers and payment details are entered on that platform's site and handled by them — they never reach this site, which has no payment form. That platform also holds the order record: your purchase, your receipt and your download. Their privacy policy governs that data.

Third-party links

Links to other sites take you off this site. Those sites have their own policies, and we don't control them.

Your rights

You can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Because this site holds nothing, the only records that exist about a purchase are held by the payment platform you bought through — their customer portal is the fastest route. Requests made through the support form are answered within 30 days, no charge for asking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version stays on this page with its date below.
