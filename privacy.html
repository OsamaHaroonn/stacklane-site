    Privacy policy

    Short version: this site has nothing to collect. The long version is below.

    What this site does not do

    • No cookies — not for ads, not for analytics, not for preferences.
    • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts.
    • No accounts, no login, no database of visitors.
    • No contact form, no newsletter and no account. Nothing you type into a tool is sent anywhere.
    • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.

    Where your input goes

    Nowhere. Every tool on this site runs in your own browser using code that is already on the page. Text you paste, numbers you enter and images you pick are read by scripts running on your device, and the result is drawn back to the same device. There is no upload step, because there is no server endpoint to upload to.

    The Content Security Policy on each page blocks any request to another origin, so if a script ever tried to send your input somewhere, the browser would refuse it.

    Files you pick

    When you choose an image or a document for a tool, the browser reads it through the local file API. The file is not uploaded, not stored on our side, and is forgotten as soon as you close or reload the tab.

    Hosting

    This site is a folder of static files served by a hosting provider and its content delivery network. As with any web server, the provider may record the request itself — the page requested, the time, your IP address and your browser's user agent — in order to deliver the page and to detect abuse. Those are the provider's operational logs: this site has no application server, does not receive them, does not combine them with anything you type into a tool, and does not use them to build a profile of you.

    There is no first-party database, no account record and no server-side storage of visitor input.

    What your browser keeps

    Two pages save a draft in your own browser's local storage so a refresh doesn't erase your work: the invoice generator and the website audit checklist. No other page writes anything to storage. That data lives on your device only and you can clear it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The invoice generator expires its drafts after seven days, because they hold names and prices.

    Advertising

    There is no advertising on this site today. The pages contain reserved spaces for ad units, but the spaces are switched off: they take no space on screen, no ad code is loaded, and nothing is requested from an advertising network.

    If advertising is switched on later, this section and the section above will be rewritten before that happens, because an ad network means a third-party script, a change to the Content Security Policy on every page, and — depending on where you are — a consent step before any cookie is set. None of that is present now.

    Your rights

    This site holds no profile of you. The hosting logs described above belong to the hosting provider and are not used to identify visitors. If you have written to the contact address, that message exists in a mailbox: you can ask for it to be deleted, and it will be.

    Changes

    If this policy changes, the current version stays on this page with its date below.
