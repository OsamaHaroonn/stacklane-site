Privacy policy

Short version: this site is built so that it has almost nothing to collect. The long version is below.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies — not for ads, not for analytics, not for "preferences".
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts.
  • No accounts, no login, no database of visitors.
  • No advertising or data brokers, because there is no data to share.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information, ever.

What stays on your device

The free tools (invoice generator, calculators) save your drafts in your own browser's local storage so a refresh doesn't erase your work. That data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Nothing is uploaded — you can disconnect from the internet and the tools keep working.

What you choose to send

If you email or message us, we receive what you wrote and your contact details, so we can reply. We use them only to answer you and to do the work you asked for. We keep project correspondence for as long as it is useful for support, then delete it on request.

Payments

The Kit is paid for through a third-party checkout provider. Card numbers and payment details are entered on that provider's site and handled by them — they never reach this site, which has no payment form at all. We receive a confirmation of the sale and your delivery email address.

Third-party links

Links to WhatsApp, PayPal or other services take you off this site. Those sites have their own policies, and we don't control them.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use. Write to our email and we'll respond within 30 days — no charge for asking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version stays on this page with its date below.
