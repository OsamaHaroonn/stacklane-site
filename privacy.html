Privacy policy

Short version: this site has nothing to collect. The long version is below.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies — not for ads, not for analytics, not for preferences.
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts.
  • No accounts, no login, no database of visitors.
  • No contact form and no inbox: nothing you type is ever sent anywhere.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.

Where your input goes

Nowhere. Every tool on this site runs in your own browser using code that is already on the page. Text you paste, numbers you enter and images you pick are read by scripts running on your device, and the result is drawn back to the same device. There is no upload step, because there is no server endpoint to upload to.

The Content Security Policy on each page blocks any request to another origin, so if a script ever tried to send your input somewhere, the browser would refuse it.

Files you pick

When you choose an image or a document for a tool, the browser reads it through the local file API. The file is not uploaded, not stored on our side, and is forgotten as soon as you close or reload the tab.

What your browser keeps

A few tools save a draft in your own browser's local storage so a refresh doesn't erase your work. That data lives on your device only and you can clear it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The invoice generator expires its drafts after seven days, because they hold names and prices.

Your rights

Since nothing about you is held, there is nothing to request. If you believe otherwise, the answer will be the same as it is here.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version stays on this page with its date below.
